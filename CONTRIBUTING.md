license that contribution under MPL-2.0 unless its destination clearly states
another license. GPL browser-player contributions under `web/` are submitted
under GPL-3.0-or-later unless their files state another compatible license.

Do not submit art, music, writing, branding, or other creative content unless
you own the necessary rights and explicitly agree in the contribution to the
project's intended terms for that work. A code contribution does not grant the
project rights to unrelated creative works, and the project's reserved-content
policy does not claim ownership of a contributor's unrelated work.

Every contributor represents that they have the right to submit their work.
Third-party material must include its complete provenance and license notice.
