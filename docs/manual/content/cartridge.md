# The Cartridge

Quintra is a color-handheld cartridge with battery-backed memory. Two memories are kept, and they do not do the same job.

When you enter a room, the run is suspended. If that champion falls, the suspended run is wiped. Death is the end of the expedition. What remains is what you learned.

A separate record keeps best score, runs, wins, fallen champions, and best win time. The title screen says it plainly: only knowledge persists.

Keep the cartridge dry. Keep it out of extreme heat and out of a closed car in the sun. If the edge contacts look dull, wipe them with a dry cloth. Do not open the shell, and do not force it into a slot.
