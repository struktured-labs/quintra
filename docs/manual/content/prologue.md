# Five Sparks

The title does not wait for a dungeon. While the five spirits stand in a row, it turns seven lines. This is the whole of that procession:

> WHEN THE SKY TORE. FIVE SPIRITS WOKE.

> FIVE ARE CHOSEN TO BEAR THE SPARKS.

> BIND THE RIFT ERE ALL NAMES FADE.

> FANG GUARDS FLAME. SCALE HOLDS STONE.

> WING READS SHADOW. FIN REMEMBERS TIDE.

> STING DEFIES ROT. FIVE SEAL THE RIFT.

> CHOOSE YOUR VESSEL. REKINDLE THE DAWN.

You do not play the only hero. You choose the vessel. Wolfkin is the fang. Sauran is the scale. Corvin is the wing. Picsean is the fin. Vespine is the sting.

The people who remain say the same things, in the rooms below.

> FANG SCALE WING FIN AND STING BORE FIRE. FIVE DEFY UNNAMING.

> FIVE SPARKS PASS ON. WE KEEP THEIR FIRE. REST BEFORE NORTH.

> ROOTS REMEMBER FIVE SPARKS BEFORE KINGS. THE SERPENT HUNGERS.

> VOID FEARS MEMORY. SPEAK NAMES ALOUD. NOTHINGNESS LISTENS.

> BELLS NAME THE LOST, SO VOID CANNOT MAKE THEM NEVER BE.

The bellkeeper, the lorekeeper, the wayfarers, and the stones along Riftwild do not agree on every cause. They agree that names are how the dark is refused. Learn the names. The next run will not draw the same rooms, but it will still answer to them.
