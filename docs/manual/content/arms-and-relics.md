# Arms and Relics

A weapon orb is permanent until you swap it. Stand on the red orb and press A. The old weapon drops where you stand. Anything that only lasts for the run is still only for the run. Death clears the pack.

## Opening weapons

Fang Forms is Wolfkin’s A: a stab with the pad, a slash when you are still, a slower cadence that speed can quicken. Tail Spike hits harder and recovers longer. Featherbarb is the returning shot. BubbleBolt is the slow pierce. Stinger is the fast strike.

Two later A weapons can come from a mini-boss or a vault, for any champion. Rift Flail is a slow three-pierce sweep, the widest of the heavy arms. Astral Spear is the opposite: one long precise thrust. Line it up. Do not spray it.

## The free B powers

Howl: eight shots and a brief ward. Stoneskin: blocks hits for a time. Raven Mark: mark one foe, ravens dive on it. Tidal Wave: three bubbles and a ward. Swarm: orbiting stingers. Each has a cooldown counted in rooms. Two rooms, or three for Raven Mark and Swarm.

## Secondaries, once you own them

The Aim page cycles only what is in the pack.

- Boomerang. The shop line is “B: stun and fetch.” It goes out, stops ordinary monsters for a moment, cuts hostile shots, and brings loose hearts, coins, magic, and relics home. Elites, mini-bosses, colossi, stock, and terrain ignore the stun. Dungeon price 30.
- Hookshot. “Latch and pull self.” Price 35. This is a weapon, not the Rift Hook that crosses holes.
- Morningstar. “Swing around you.” Price 30.
- Bomb Bag. The pack prints that name. The shelf says “1 MP: throw or drop.” Stand still to drop, or hold a direction to throw. Price 30. The consumable Rift Bomb is a different, single-use pack item: it breaks cover and bursts the nearby lanes.

## Relics that change the body

Iron Heart, maximum health +2. Speed Ring, speed +1. Power Stone, attack +1. Tough Skin, defense +1. Lucky Coin, luck +2. Mana Gem, maximum magic +2. Ward Charm, defense +1 and luck +1, and it is a real ward: a curse from a monster is stopped, and a curse you accept from a Wildcard is softened. Swift Fang, speed +1 and attack +1. Hunter’s Eye, luck +3. Vampiric Sigil, attack +1 and maximum health +1, and a heal on every fifth kill.

## Tools and strange verbs

Rift Bomb, Echo Chime, and Mirror Shard are single uses. The chime stills bullets and shows a secret and the nearby paths. The shard turns hostile shots back on whoever fired them.

Blast Seed makes the first impact of a shot bloom. Rift Lens makes every third primary attack a heavy beam. Both are found in Hollow Riftwild caches, not in an ordinary shop, and the cartridge tells you they belong to Hollow.

The shop also names wares beyond that list. Read the shelf. The words on the glass are the contract: a heart refill, an alternate A weapon, “HP for ATK+SPD” on the Glass Fang pact, one revive on the Phoenix thread, magic plus a weapon surge, a fork on every fourth A, a shot that bounces once, a counter-volley when you are hit, B and magic back on every fifth kill, and surplus hearts turned into magic.

Weapon Surge is a temporary burst of the primary, on the order of fifteen seconds, not a new weapon.

## Waygear

Owned gear is always on. You do not equip one and silence the others.

- Titan Glove. Pass rune boulders. Sauran can already do this.
- Tide Raft. Cross rivers and lakes. Picsean can already swim.
- Rift Hook. Cross holes and gaps. Corvin can already. On a collapsing floor, anyone else drops to zero health. Corvin, or a champion with the Hook, stumbles off instead.
- Worldglass. SELECT+B in Riftwild shears you between Waking and Hollow. The same cell reforms around you. If the new ground would occupy your body, the cartridge looks for the nearest safe tile.

The Glove, the Raft, and the Hook are also the three Will-tier discoveries.
