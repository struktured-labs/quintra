# Before You Play

A small number of people may feel dizzy, nauseated, or disoriented when they watch flashing lights or dense moving patterns. Quintra’s colossi throw those patterns on purpose. If your eyes tire, or the room seems to tilt, stop. Rest. Play in a lit room, and keep the screen a comfortable distance away.

This page is general guidance in the old booklet tradition. It is not a medical finding, a safety certification, or a promise that any particular player will be unaffected.

Take breaks. The road will still be there.
