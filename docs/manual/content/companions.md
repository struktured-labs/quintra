# Road Echoes

One run may find one Road Echo, chosen from the seed, inside an optional cache that does not look like a cache. After that, the echo stays for the expedition. It follows. If you leave it too far behind, it steps back to your side. It can loose a small shot toward a threat. The gift you ask for is the larger thing.

Open the Compass with SELECT. Press A to ask. The echo refuses while it is resting, and it refuses an ask it cannot fulfill. A full health bar will not take a mend. A full magic bar will not take a spark.

The three introductions, as they introduce themselves:

- Hearth Echo. “Your heart summoned me. I mend two half HP.” That is one heart. Select the map, then A.
- Aether Echo. “Your spark summoned me. I restore two magic.” Same ask.
- Way Echo. “The road called me. I reveal near paths.” The ask also grants a short invulnerable beat. In a dungeon it marks neighboring cells. In Riftwild it marks the exits of the screen you stand on. In a town it opens a wider reveal.

They are not a second champion. Do not plan a boss around the shot. Plan it around the ask, and around the fact that you only meet one of them.
