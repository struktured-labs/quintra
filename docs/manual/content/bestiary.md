# Bestiary

Names are the ones in the content tables, abbreviations included. Health is the table’s number, not the colossus bar. A mini-boss and a giant use larger bodies than the rank and file. Where a creature “keeps to” a dungeon, that is the stage pool, not a promise that every room rolls it.

## Crystal Caverns

- **B. Crawler.** Health 8. Walks. Weak to fire in the table.
- **Hornet.** Health 7. Chases. On Normal a group takes flanks around you, and it keeps a light aimed shot that Easy omits.
- **Rope.** Health 8. A short tell, then a rush.
- **Flutterbat.** Health 6. Walks, and it is small enough to start in awkward gaps. Also common in the Hollow and the Mire.
- **GloomLeech.** Health 10. Chases. Also in the Hollow.
- **Shard Crab.** Health 9. Guards, then rushes.
- **Facet Ram.** Health 30. Walks. The cavern’s own notes call it a directional armor problem, with a bad lane behind it. The table gives it no elemental weakness.

## Verdant Hollow

- **Orc.** Health 32. A long tell, then a charge. Also in Ember and the Temple.
- **Vine Coil.** Health 10. Orbits and fires. The slow opposite-pair lane of the Hollow.

## Ember Depths

- **Bomber.** Health 24. Walks, and the real threat is the death. It bursts in four directions. Kill it from a diagonal. Also in the Vault and the Mire.
- **CinderMaw.** Health 32. Slow. Fires a three-way fan. Cross after the fan, not during it.
- **CinderKite.** Health 12. A moving shooter. The light fan to the Maw’s heavy one.
- **Fold Star.** Health 18. Opens, then closes. While it is open it throws invulnerable echoes. It returns in Shadow Keep, Bloodmoon, and the Sanctum.
- **Rift Ooze.** Health 14. Walks. On death it cracks into two small crawler fragments. Also the Mire’s common body, and the Sanctum’s.

## Frost Vault

- **Wisp.** Health 7. Fires a single shot. Also the Mire and Shadow Keep.
- **Skeleton.** Health 10. Chases.
- **Sentry.** Health 12. Does not walk. It rotates and fires. Shoot it between turns.
- **Frost Lancer.** Health 13. A long tell, then a rush down a lane.
- **Mirror Moth.** Health 10. It steps the opposite way from you, then fires a slow bolt back toward you. Standing still stops the step. It does not stop the shot.

## Toxic Mire

- **Mire Spore.** Health 13. Sits still. If you come close it arms, then blows. Bait the fuse and leave.
- **Bog Toad.** Health 15. A tell, then a fast pounce.
- **Rift Cantor.** Health 13. A summoner with a visible tell. It is the thin slice at the top of the Frost, Mire, Shadow, Temple, Bloodmoon, and Sanctum pools. Kill it before the room fills.

## Shadow Keep

- **Shade.** Health 9. Vanishes and reappears beside you. Also the Temple and Bloodmoon.
- **RuneLantern.** Health 15. A shooter. Also the Sanctum.
- **PrismSkitt.** Health 14. Orbits and fires.
- **BrambleSpr.** Health 11. A wider, slower orbit than the skitter.

## Golden Temple

- **Echo Guard.** Health 18. Guards, then rushes.
- **Dread Bell.** Health 17. A shooter. A bell tagged as a warden pays a weapon orb the way a mini-boss does. Ordinary bells do not. Also Bloodmoon and the Sanctum.
- **RiftWarden.** Health 16. A shooter. The arch wardens of Riftwild are a different duty: they hold a sleeping gate. Also Bloodmoon and the Sanctum.
- **Sunwheel.** Health 14. Holds a tight orbit and marks a changing opposite lane.

## Bloodmoon

- **Warlock.** Health 28. A shooter with real health. Do not trade into the cast.
- **Dusk Midge.** Health 10. A fast shooter. The Sanctum’s pool replaces this band with the Void Halo.

## Void Sanctum

- **Void Halo.** Health 12. A wide, slow orbit. The pair of lanes is the puzzle. Do not chase the rim.

## On the roster, off the ordinary mix

- **S.Sentinel.** Health 50 in the table. Walks. The same enemy id, when flagged as a giant, is how a colossus is marked. Killing the giant advances the stage. Killing a mini-boss does not.
- **Dread Reaper.** Health 30. Listed as a walker. It is not in the nine stage pools. Treat a room that contains one as a special route, not as a random crawler.

Elites and the occasional empowered body hit harder and pay better. The alpha rule is in The Road. Learn silhouettes. The game is short on names once the shooting starts, and the wrong target is how runs end.
