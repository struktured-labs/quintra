# Colossi

Each dungeon ends in one. The window grows a bar. The floor of the arena is kept quiet so the body can be read. You cannot hurt them the way you hurt a crawler and then walk away: they have phases, and the last quarter of the bar is a crisis. A mini-boss earlier in the dungeon is not this fight.

The advice in quotes is what wayfarers will tell you. Take it. They have died of the alternative.

## Crystal Colossus

![A cluster of cyan and white crystal](images/boss-crystal.png)

Caption: Crystal Colossus. The first pattern lesson.

Crystal Caverns. It warns, then jumps among distant wells, and leaves a punish while it settles. “Sidestep his aim. Slip through rings. Strike as he rests.” The bar is capped at 220. Do not try to end it in one corridor.

## Storm Serpent

![A magenta coil with a gold head](images/boss-serpent.png)

Caption: Storm Serpent. It feeds, grows, and coils.

Verdant Hollow. It eats, grows, constricts, discharges, and contracts. “The serpent feeds. Deny four storms. Flee the full coil.” Cap 240. The body is purple, magenta, and yellow against the green room so you can see which part of the coil is the part that kills you.

## Kilnback Pack and Cinder Rex

![An ember beast with a long jaw](images/boss-rex.png)

Caption: Cinder Rex, after the kilnback pack.

Ember Depths is two acts. Five furnace segments come first. What remains is Cinder Rex. You are meant to hit opened vents, after a formation or a committed attack, not to scratch a closed hide. The wayfarer still says: “Cinder maws hold. Three burning lanes. Cross after the fan.” Cap 240 for the whole fight. Endless Ember keeps that same cap.

## Frost Spider

![A pale spider and a small web](images/boss-spider.png)

Caption: Frost Spider. Blink, then the web.

Frost Vault. A warning flash, then a blink to your flank, then a beat before the web resolves. “Watch for the blink. Then cross the web. Do not wait center.” Cap 150. That is enough to see more than one cycle, and not enough to be careless.

## Mire Heart

![A yellow-green heart with roots beneath it](images/boss-mire.png)

Caption: Mire Heart. Roots, then the crush.

Toxic Mire. It pulses outward, then contracts into a crushing advance. Roots can hold it in place. Shots pass over the root overlay. The ring starts only after the roots clear. “Leave the pulse before mire swells. Return after impact.” The bar can run to 255.

## Shadow Reaper

![A dark cloak and a pale blade](images/boss-reaper.png)

Caption: Shadow Reaper. It leaves, and it comes back on a flank.

Shadow Keep. It hunts, telegraphs, and re-enters beside you. “Gold floor falls. Falls end the run. Rift Hook saves.” That second lesson is the room, not the cloak. Corvin does not need the Hook. Everyone else does, or the floor is the end. The bar can run to 255.

## Sun Golem

![A gold disc with a dark core and eight rays](images/boss-sun.png)

Caption: Sun Golem. Move after the ring.

Golden Temple. “Golem slams twice. Move after the ring. Corners buy time.” The cap is 230. Do not admire the hammer from the center.

## Blood Hydra

![Three red heads on one body](images/boss-hydra.png)

Caption: Blood Hydra. Three heads, one gap.

Bloodmoon. The heads weave more slowly than the Serpent, on a different rhythm, with staggered streams. “Hydra heads weave. Cut through the gap. Do not chase edges.” Cap 150. Weaving is the fight. Chasing a single head into the corner is how the other two find you.

## Void Lord

![A dark mass with a green core](images/boss-void.png)

Caption: Void Lord. The core moves. The collapse does not forgive.

Void Sanctum. The weak point jumps between anchors on the body and knits health back slowly, about one point on a patient clock. World Collapse announces one safe pocket. “Void heals slowly. Press every opening. Flee the collapse.” Cap 220. This is the ninth, and the win the cartridge records.

Oaths unlock in this same order. By the Sanctum you should have eight arts already, and the ninth when it falls. If you reach the Void Lord with an empty pack and a habit of standing still, the booklet has no further comfort to offer.
