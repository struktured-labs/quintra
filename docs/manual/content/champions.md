# Champions

Numbers below are the Normal start, before relics. Hearts are health divided by two. Each champion also walks one kind of bad ground without a tool. The matching Waygear lets the others walk it too, and the first three tools raise the Will cap.

## Wolfkin

![Wolfkin, a tan wolf-champion with tall ears](images/hero-wolfkin.png)

Caption: Wolfkin. Fang guards flame. Blade fighter.

Seven hearts. Magic 4. Attack 2. Defense 1. Speed 6. The select screen calls the role a blade fighter, the weapon “stab slash dash,” the power “8 shots brief ward,” and the trait fast movement. The content names are Fang Forms on A and Howl on B. Howl is an eight-way burst and a very short ward, not a room-clearing stun. Wolfkin walks thorn tiles. A movement perk sits on the class; the screen’s words for it are FAST MOVEMENT.

## Sauran

![Sauran, a broad green scaled champion](images/hero-sauran.png)

Caption: Sauran. Scale holds stone. Armored tank.

Eight hearts. Magic 3. Attack 2. Defense 2. Speed 5. Role: armored tank. Weapon: heavy tail strike, named Tail Spike, a short blow with bonus damage on the hit. Power: Stoneskin, a timed guard against shots and bodies. Trait: health regen. The slow recovery is already baked into the starting body. Sauran pushes rune boulders. The Titan Glove teaches that trick to everyone else.

## Corvin

![Corvin, a violet crow-champion with a pale beak](images/hero-corvin.png)

Caption: Corvin. Wing reads shadow. Ranged hunter.

Seven hearts. Magic 8. Attack 2. Defense 1. Speed 5. Role: ranged hunter. Weapon: returning feather, named Featherbarb. Power: Raven Mark, which marks one live foe so ravens focus it. Trait: show enemy health. Corvin crosses holes. The Rift Hook is that mercy for the other four. Low defense is the price of the returning arc. Do not stand in the pattern while you wait for the feather to come home.

## Picsean

![Picsean, a river-blue champion with a crest](images/hero-picsean.png)

Caption: Picsean. Fin remembers tide. Magic caster.

Seven hearts. Magic 10. Attack 1. Defense 1. Speed 5. The lowest attack on the roster. Role: magic caster. Weapon: piercing bubble, named BubbleBolt, slow and piercing. Power: Tidal Wave, a three-bubble wall and a long guard. Trait: fast magic regen. Picsean swims. The Tide Raft is the same door for the others. Spacing is the whole class. The bolt is slow. You are not.

## Vespine

![Vespine, an amber hornet-champion with small wings and a stinger](images/hero-vespine.png)

Caption: Vespine. Sting defies rot. Fast striker.

Six hearts and a half. Magic 5. Attack 4. Defense 1. Speed 7. The fastest body, and the sharpest base attack. Role: fast striker. Weapon: Stinger, a fast strike with a luck bonus on the weapon itself. Power: Swarm, rotating stingers and an attack ward. Trait: strong elements. The content perk is poison synergy. Vespine crosses vents. Close range is the danger. Swarm’s ward is there so the finish does not have to be suicidal.
