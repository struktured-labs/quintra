# Controls

The pad moves. It also aims.

## Hold Aim

Hold Aim is the default. Any direction, including the four diagonals, becomes your aim. Release the pad and you stop walking, but the aim stays. The next A, B, Oath, or full Will fires that way until you press a new direction.

Hold up and right, let go, then strike. The shot goes northeast. Press down afterward, let go, and the next still shot goes south.

A new champion begins aimed south. Aim is not written into the suspend. It does survive an ordinary change of room until you replace it.

Classic aim is the other setting. Diagonals collapse toward the vertical heading. Switch modes on the Aim page: press A.

## The buttons

- A is the primary weapon. Beside a red weapon orb, A swaps that weapon in and leaves the old one on the ground.
- Let go of A and Will charges. The next A, once Will is stored, spends it on a heavier form of the weapon you hold. Talking does not spend Will. A shield or Mute pauses the charge. Menus pause the world.
- B is the selected hero power. It is free, and it must cool down. On the Aim page, Up and Down choose a secondary B weapon you already own: Boomerang, Hookshot, Morningstar, or the Bomb Bag. The cycle returns to the champion’s own power.
- A and B together, while magic is not full, fires the Oath you have selected. The cartridge subtracts 1 magic. The Combat page prints “A+B OATH: 2 MP.” Trust the meter, not that line: the cost in the program is 1.
- A and B together at full magic is Spirit Convergence. It does not charge the 1-magic cost. It empties the meter, throws an eight-way volley, and holds the ascended stance for a long beat. Mute blocks Oaths, Convergence, and Will.
- Double-tap a direction to dodge-dash. The dash grants a brief invulnerable moment.
- START opens the Pack. From the Pack, SELECT cycles Aim and B weapon, then Waygear, then Status, then Combat, then back to the Pack. START or B puts you back in the room. Up and Down on the Pack change the selected Oath, once any Oath is unlocked.
- SELECT in the world opens the Compass. If a Road Echo travels with you, A on that map asks it for aid. SELECT and B together, once you own Worldglass, shears Riftwild between Waking and Hollow.

The title’s help compresses that cycle. It prints Pack, Gear, and Status, and leaves Aim and Combat to the longer path above.

Oaths begin locked. Each colossus you defeat unlocks one more, in campaign order, up to nine. Their names, as printed: Shard Wake, Root Call, Cinderstep, Still Wave, Miasma, Gloam Shift, Sun Turn, Red Harvest, Rift Exchange.
