# Quintra v0.18.88 — Rift Bazaar

ROM SHA-256: `b5bd1fff587e63e96b2207ba311152c4404a794f66e0bfc322eaada0d76cfba0`

Dungeon merchants now create an actual roguelike build decision. Every safe
shop keeps a recovery counter and a seed-stable, champion-attuned sealed
relic, then independently rolls one build offer and one tactical offer.

Build offers:

- **Vampiric Sigil** — sustained fifth-kill healing;
- **Weapon Trade** — a run-changing Rift Flail or Astral Spear;
- **Glass Fang** — sacrifice one max heart for +2 ATK and +1 SPD;
- **Echo Prism** — every fourth primary A attack forks into two half-damage
  side lanes.

Tactical offers:

- **Surge Tonic** — the established fifteen-second class-shaped weapon burst;
- **Cartographer's Chart** — reveal the full active dungeon;
- **Phoenix Cord** — consumed once to intercept lethal damage and revive at
  half health with a safe recovery beat;
- **Spirit Draught** — fill MP and start Surge, immediately enabling the
  full-MP A+B Spirit Convergence decision.

The complete 4×4 catalog is derived from the run seed without consuming combat
RNG. A paid sealed relic now exposes a champion-attuned payload fixed at spawn
instead of rolling an arbitrary stat only after coins are spent. Dungeon shops
use four spaced counters with floor prices, persistent sale tags, category
palettes, and the existing nearby semantic HUD icon.

The Spirit Compass also receives a legibility pass. Its 16×16 purple objective
node is now a centered exclamation mark rather than a diagonal rune that could
read as a stray `P`. The Riftwild legend correctly renders `RIFT`; shared tile
slots previously allowed the in-play `P/N/H` district alphabet to overwrite
its `R/F` glyphs and produce `PNIHT`. Live pixel tests now pin the distinct
R/F strokes, all 20→30 dungeon nodes, the current marker, objective, loot,
nonlinear link, and pre-entry amber boss node.

Finally, entering Riftwild or a village cancels any carried dungeon
district-label fade. That timer could otherwise expire after arrival and
stream grass over the permanent `MARKET` landmark. The intended red palette
for the village's special weapon counter is also restored.

Verification includes all sixteen seeded shop combinations; real purchases
and effects for Surge, Glass Fang, Echo Prism, Phoenix Cord, and Spirit
Draught; the connected three-screen village; Compass visual semantics; the
26/26 mGBA smoke suite; Rust content tests; cartridge layout; and fixed ROM
budget gates. Six native mGBA training states were captured at five-minute
intervals through minute thirty and all cold-load successfully. The retained
controller run reaches stage two, then stalls alive in room 38; those snapshots
are valid deep-test fixtures, not a claim of full-route controller completion.
