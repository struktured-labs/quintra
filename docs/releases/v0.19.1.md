# Quintra v0.19.1 — Relics & Refrains

This milestone makes run upgrades readable in the world and gives every
dungeon and Colossus a longer musical identity.

- Twenty-one native 8x8 item silhouettes replace the generic recolored oval:
  Iron Heart, Speed Ring, Power Stone, Tough Skin, Lucky Coin, Mana Gem, Ward
  Charm, Swift Fang, Hunter Eye, Blood Sigil, weapon, chart, Surge, Phoenix
  Cord, Echo Prism, Ricochet Rune, Thorn Crown, War Drum, Moon Flask, Spirit
  Draught, and the progression Rift Sigil.
- Loose drops, Farfold caches, mutually exclusive director boons, weapon
  rewards, progression fixtures, town stock, and procedural dungeon merchants
  all share that shape-first vocabulary. Palette still reinforces category,
  while the existing proximity HUD supplies the exact name and price.
- A live town/market contract proves all 21 loaded OBJ tiles are distinct and
  that every generated 6x6 merchant-catalog combination keeps its semantic
  world art through real purchase behavior.
- All nine dungeon themes and all nine matching boss themes expand from 32
  melody rows / 8 bass changes to 64 melody rows / 16 bass changes.
- Each new second half is an authored response rather than a repeated first
  half. Crystal, Verdant, Ember, Frost, Mire, Shadow, Golden, Bloodmoon, and
  Void also vary tempo, pulse duty, articulation, use of rests, and bass mix.
- The compact score format stores notes as one-byte symbols and expands them
  through one shared hardware-frequency table. Stage scores live in bank 6
  and boss scores in bank 7; the sequencer restores the active gameplay bank
  after each row.
- True rests now silence the previous pulse or bass note instead of letting
  sparse phrases smear into the same drone. Ordinary room transitions still
  continue the current musical row rather than restarting the track.
- The creator worksheet accepts the new 64/16 gameplay format as well as
  compact 32/8 title or ending drafts, supports the full C3–B3 bass register,
  and emits the correct wave-channel frequency values.
- The refreshed title, shop, village, Ember, boss-gallery, and 174-frame
  gameplay reel are bound to this exact ROM hash.

The v0.19.1 cartridge passed the complete static, live-ROM, collision,
progression, item/economy, boss, enemy, controller-policy, deterministic replay,
and media suite. A fixed-seed 90,000-frame controller run crossed the first
village into the next dungeon, and all 460 external Normal/Easy
stage/champion checkpoints load against this build.

The ROM remains a 128 KiB CGB-only MBC5 + 32 KiB battery-RAM cartridge. Every
switchable bank retains at least the required 1 KiB development floor.
