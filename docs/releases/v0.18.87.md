# Quintra v0.18.87 — Fivefold Depths

ROM SHA-256: `6e35730a17baa888b4880ad3e1ea83b953f980f047156d9f14c11e2a79f3221a`

Attended testing correctly identified that nominally large stages could still
feel compact. The first dungeon already contains 20 nodes—17 consecutive
248×248 fields plus its compact merchant, sanctuary, and Colossus cadence—and
later stages reach 30 nodes, but their distant acreage reused one visual
grammar. This release gives every dungeon five named physical districts:
**GATE, LOWER, DEEP, INNER,** and **HEART**.

Each six-cell Compass row now changes both its brief arrival callout and its
complete field architecture:

- Gate uses long outer pylons;
- Lower uses offset retaining walls and a broad weave;
- Deep uses a four-gated sunken ring;
- Inner uses crossing processional walls and wards;
- Heart uses a broken nested keep before the merchant, sanctuary, and
  Colossus cadence.

The silhouettes extend into the eastern and southern acreage beyond the old
160×136 viewport. Paired gaps, a final cardinal cross, and preserved encounter
aprons keep every generated exit and combat lane reachable. Crossing a
north/south district threshold sounds a calm A4→D5 bell while the stage music
and streamed 31×31 transition continue. The name lasts two seconds only on a
new depth or stage, then restores the exact generated floor beneath it; the
Compass remains the persistent source of district position.

This milestone changes perceptual geography rather than counters: room totals,
required objective distance, topology, enemies, projectiles, bosses, rewards,
and both difficulty modes are unchanged. Live-ROM tests prove that all five
district fields differ materially, distant aprons remain connected, exact
tile-native callouts fade back to generated terrain across rotated seams and
Compass resume, and the guaranteed Gate→Lower transition remains reversible
without blanking the LCD or hiding the champion.

The final composition audit checks every large depth row across all nine stage
archetypes and requires every 2×2 body-valid position to belong to the central
connected field. It directly pins the northeast Gate ruin and Inner lower
cloister combinations that exposed overlapping-wall pockets during
development. The fixed-seed controller campaign subsequently traverses the
complete required route, defeats all nine Colossi, and reaches room 220
victory. Its cold recorded-input replay passes in 158,896 frames with 16 HP
remaining.

Walking, dashes, and damage knockback now share one six-probe/full-body
collision contract. Knockback cannot create the exceptional centre-pillar
overlap that previously let a one-pixel controller route tunnel through
scenery, and ordinary input has no overlap exception to exploit. Live-ROM
coverage pins lower-centre contact for crates and one-tile pillars, dash
contact, synthetic embedded-state containment, and the east/south field edges
at x/y=232.

Dungeon merchants now roll a genuinely run-shaping featured shelf from seven
families: permanent vitality, temporary class-shaped Surge, vampiric sustain,
attack, mana, full dungeon charting, or an alternate A-weapon. Healing and the
procedural relic shelf remain familiar anchors. The roll is seed-stable and
does not consume combat RNG; its physical palette and nearby HUD glyph expose
the category and exact price before contact. Live-ROM coverage enumerates all
seed outcomes and completes a real priced Surge purchase.
