# Quintra v0.18.90 — True Footing

Generated terrain now agrees with the champion's physical scale. The 16×16
champion and responsive 12-pixel feet box are unchanged; misleading 8-pixel
slots have been removed from the traversal language instead.

- Colonnades use paired piers with genuinely body-wide openings.
- Wide-court clusters join into coherent masses instead of diagonal slits.
- Ceremonial crests use a readable two-solid/two-accent rhythm.
- A deterministic final pass closes only isolated one-tile floor cells caught
  between permanent scenery, both before and after room-role overlays.
- Reachability scratch metadata is masked during that pass, covering deep
  dungeon wings and the full 31×31 scrolling field.
- Crystals now sit on a full-width pedestal, visually communicating their
  solid collision.
- Wide streamed seams perform a settled-state battery commit, preserving the
  destination room, coins, and score together across a real power cycle.

Live-ROM regression coverage samples Verdant, Ember, Frost, Toxic, Shadow,
Golden, Bloodmoon, and Void silhouettes, including later wing thresholds.
Every authored graph exit remains body-reachable. A separate full-field
contract checks the complete 31×31 court and its reciprocal scrolling arrival
seams, while C/Rust procgen parity remains 12/12 across ordinary, Rift,
miniboss, shop, and sanctuary roles. Preflight also cold-boots a separate
emulator process from the emitted 32 KiB battery image and verifies a
distinctive room/player payload.

The milestone retains the v0.18.89 Living Bazaar catalog, corrected late-game
Colossus damage curve, 6×5 tile-native Spirit Compass, nonlinear Rift travel,
five-district dungeon expeditions, villages, Riftwild overworld, and nine
large scrolling-arena Colossi.
