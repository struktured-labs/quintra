# Quintra v0.18.82 — Pocket Atlas

The SELECT Compass is now a screen-filling 6×5 abstract dungeon map. Each
room grows from one 8×8 symbol to a 16×16 metasquare, and corridors span the
full node edge. The complete 20–30-room stage footprint therefore dominates
the LCD instead of sharing it with a six-line vertical legend.

Only three semantic marks are needed: cyan arrow means YOU, violet diamond
means the next GOAL, and amber skull means BOSS. Their one-line bottom key
leaves the route itself readable. Dim unknown rooms and corridors still
brighten as they are explored; the stage heading, objective branch, staged
Sigil/Waystone/Warden cues, nonlinear Rift edge, sanctuary reveal, and all
four late-game exploration bytes remain driven by live cartridge state.

Boss commitment retains three independent warnings. Entering the sanctuary
reveals the adjacent amber skull on the Compass, the actual threshold uses a
unique 16×16 amber skull seal, and approaching that seal produces a low roar
and screen tremor.

Live-ROM verification covers the opening 20-room footprint, the room-one
junction, the sanctuary and boss reveal, semantic CGB colors, the reversible
Rift shortcut, staged objectives, and the complete 30-room finale. It also
rechecks all nine stage/boss music pairs, Normal/Easy world parity, and all
460 five-champion checkpoint combinations.

All 460 PyBoy fixtures and all 460 native mGBA states were regenerated against
the release ROM. Six additional native mGBA training states, recorded every
five emulated minutes through minute 30, cold-load successfully; the last
reaches stage 6 with five Colossi defeated. A cold replay of the exact
175,154-frame campaign input trace reaches the ending with all nine Colossi
down and 16 HP.
