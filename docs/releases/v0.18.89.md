# Quintra v0.18.89 — Living Bazaar

Dungeon merchants now offer a deterministic 6×6 catalog of build and tactical
choices. Four new relics change live play:

- Ricochet Rune rebounds each primary or signature attack once from stone.
- Thorn Crown answers a damaging hit with a four-lane counter-volley.
- War Drum restores one MP and immediately readies B every fifth kill.
- Moon Flask converts a surplus full-health heart drop into one MP.

Each offer has distinct counter color and HUD iconography. Shops keep their
healing and class-attuned relic anchors, and their two featured shelves skip a
unique relic already carried rather than presenting an unusable duplicate.
Live-ROM coverage enumerates all 36 seed-stable combinations and performs real
purchases and behavioral checks for every new hook.

Ricochet ownership is cached only when a run starts, resumes, or buys the rune;
ordinary fire does not rescan the eight-slot inventory. Its runtime bit is
separate from Spirit Convergence's chord marker. This preserves the established
147/180 dense-room performance sample and prevents rebound shots from being
misclassified as Convergence hits against a Colossus.

The same milestone corrects the late Normal Colossus damage curve found by the
progression-matched curriculum. Repeatable giant projectiles now cap at three
half-hearts while boss HP, motion, cadence, projectile density, and mixed
speeds remain unchanged. Void Sanctum's arena-wide World Collapse still
requires its announced safe corner, but a miss costs four half-hearts rather
than allowing two scripted pulses to delete a full-health run.

The corrected controller curriculum now responds to that visible corner cue:
four of five baseline Normal champions survive the full one-minute Void Lord
sample, compared with all five dying around nine seconds in v0.18.88. Across
all 45 champion/stage matches, Normal improves from 9 clears and 14 survivals
to 13 clears and 21 survivals while the final boss remains unbeaten by these
starter-like checkpoint kits inside one minute.

The cartridge now deliberately uses the eighth MBC5 ROM bank for independent
shop, HUD-art, and specialist-enemy modules. Every established gameplay bank
retains at least the one-KiB development floor, and the new content bank keeps
more than thirteen KiB free.

Release preflight proves a byte-identical clean rebuild, valid 128 KiB
MBC5/CGB cartridge headers and checksums, cold battery-SRAM resume, hash-bound
media, and a controller-only Picsean Easy victory/replay in 198,443 frames.
