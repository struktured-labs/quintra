# Quintra v0.20.17 — Quiet Pressure

Quiet Pressure makes Normal mode more consequential, gives the Riftwild score
room to breathe, and explains every combat/equipment verb on the cartridge.

- START cycles Pack, Waygear, Status, and a new Combat reference page.
- The Combat page explains A weapon swaps, free rechargeable B signatures,
  2-MP A+B Oath Arts, full-MP Convergence, and three-second Will MAX charging.
- Waygear clearly identifies the one active equipment slot and its controls.
- Normal pack alphas only restore health at or below half HP; above that they
  pay coins. True elites retain their recovery reward and Easy is unchanged.
- Normal random half-heart drops fall from about 18% to 12%.
- Shooter cadence tightens modestly at stages 1, 4, and 7 without adding HP,
  contact damage, or more bodies.
- Waking and Hollow Riftwild each gain a six-section calm midpoint: sparse
  sustained melody and a complete bass withdrawal beneath a patient pulse
  before the final thematic rebuild.

The release rebuilds all 460 deterministic cartridge checkpoints and carries
live-ROM coverage for the full Pack page cycle, status-timer pause, Riftwild
bridge arrangement, elite/alpha rewards, and Normal/Easy world parity.
