# Quintra v0.18.85 — Farfold Reliquaries

ROM SHA-256: `139313eecd90a0ee1244b66bede90bfc5447eb2aaab9e939e73cb6fbc55dbe74`

Generated dungeon scale now has an optional destination worth exploring.
Every stage selects one non-fixture dead-end as its Farfold Cache, rotating
among local depth bands 6, 12, and 18 across the approved procedural folds.
The cache never replaces a Sigil, Warden, Waystone, nonlinear Rift, merchant,
sanctuary, or Colossus room.

Its crystal-pylon reliquary occupies the southeast sector of a real 248×248
scrolling district, beyond both former viewport seams and away from the direct
cardinal route. The relic persists until collected, grants a permanent
class-affinity build item, and uses dungeon state to prevent backtracking from
spawning another copy.

The Spirit Compass exposes this optional branch with a dedicated gold chest
and a compact `LOOT` legend. Horizontal districts are numbered 1–5 at the
right edge, making the 6×5 graph read as increasing depth rather than one
undifferentiated rectangle. Claimed loot returns to an ordinary explored node.

The refreshed macro fold set guarantees an eligible optional dead end while
retaining eighteen sampled seed/stage graph signatures, one objective loop,
9–19 direct boss distance, and the existing 19–31-transition mandatory
Sigil/Warden/Waystone route. Normal and Easy still share geometry and route
logic.

Live-ROM verification proves:

- the Compass advertises the correct generated cache cell;
- the altar and relic exist beyond both old viewport seams;
- the relic does not expire while the player explores;
- collection changes the permanent run inventory;
- the LOOT marker clears after collection;
- returning to the reliquary cannot farm another relic.

No room count, mandatory objective, enemy HP, encounter roster, boss balance,
difficulty value, town cadence, or service-room placement increases.
The dense hostile-projectile path also avoids redundant player-only material
checks, raising the hardware-paced stress result from 143 to 147 of 180 frames.
