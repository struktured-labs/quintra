# Quintra v0.18.86 — Footprint Compass

ROM SHA-256: `3ff0acc51f6e33f4155152d5032f6706ed25e9b0203593ebcbd991780e5f41d1`

The SELECT Compass now shows walked and unvisited rooms with different shapes,
not merely different green brightness. Explored rooms have a bright solid
frame and stippled interior. Unknown rooms remain recognizable squares but use
empty dashed frames. Bright corridors join the explored footprints while the
unwalked route stays dim.

The complete 6×5 procedural graph remains compressed into one tile-native
screen. Current room, sequential goal, hinted Colossus, optional Farfold loot,
nonlinear Rift, and depth-band markers keep their existing semantic colors and
large 16×16 glyphs. Rendered-image coverage proves that explored interiors and
unknown dashed edges remain distinct independently of tile IDs.

The class selector now makes the testing assist explicit:
`NORMAL SELECT>EASY` changes to `EASY SELECT>NORMAL` after pressing SELECT.
Normal remains the default.

The accompanying v0.18.85 balance audit measured:

- zero deaths across 45 stage-entry and 45 mid-stage ordinary-room samples;
- 20/45 resolved stage entries and 33/45 resolved mid-stage courts;
- 8/45 cleared and 12/45 survived isolated Normal Colossus matchups.

Those diagnostics locate the current automated difficulty spike in the boss
curriculum rather than ordinary-room lethality. No hero, enemy, projectile,
Colossus, encounter, procgen, reward, or Normal/Easy balance value changes in
this milestone; attended play remains authoritative before further Normal
tuning.
