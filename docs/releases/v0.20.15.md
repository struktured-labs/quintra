# Quintra v0.20.15 — Clear Choice

This patch makes every living-dungeon boon choice physically honest.

- Elite and Hold completions continue to offer two mutually exclusive build
  boons, with the existing grace window preventing an automatic selection.
- The compact reward flood now rejects floor cells beneath full-height
  pillars and push blocks, matching the collision rule used by live champion
  movement. A reward can no longer look available while the champion's upper
  body is blocked from reaching it.
- The live-ROM director regression explores the cartridge's exact six-point
  feet/body movement space and proves that both spawned boons are collectible.
- Shared major-reward, Rift Sigil, Farfold Cache, miniboss-placement, stage
  topology, cartridge-layout, and checksum contracts remain green.
- The Field Archive now indexes all 35 registered monsters, including visible
  Facet Ram and Dread Reaper portraits guarded against blank capture frames.
- The release ROM and all README-facing captures are regenerated together and
  bound to the same cartridge hash.

The fix is deliberately placement-only: boon effects, class-attuned reward
pools, mutual exclusion, and encounter pacing are unchanged.
