# Quintra v0.18.91 — Living Arsenal

This milestone raises encounter density across the complete run and makes the
run's accumulated equipment visible in play.

- Ordinary compact combat rooms roll 3–6 hostiles in stage one, 4–7 in stages
  two and three, and 5–8 from stage four onward. Scrolling courts add one body
  for their larger footprint.
- Nine guaranteed-open court anchors and four Riftwild trail anchors distribute
  the denser population instead of stacking bodies on repeated coordinates.
- Relics and Colossus clears advance each champion through blue, red, and
  pearl-gold equipment tiers while preserving class silhouettes and accents.
- Gear refreshes after live pickups, purchases, weapon swaps, boss clears,
  doors, portals, START resume, and external checkpoint entry.
- Fang Forms, organic Tail Spike/Stinger, Rift Flail, and Astral Spear use
  distinct tiles and weapon palettes. Slash, heavy-chain, and thrust attacks
  have separate register-level SFX.
- Stoneskin and Tidal Guard have distinct stone/water art. Tidal Guard starts
  broader, and both catch radii visibly grow with equipment tier.
- A live-ROM regression proves all four champion palette states, sword versus
  Flail VRAM identity, and Stoneskin versus Tidal Guard VRAM identity. Density
  sampling covers all nine stages and the 31×31 court distribution contract.
- A 20-defect stability sweep makes merchant transactions atomic, migrates
  every accepted legacy suspend layout, saturates lifetime and 16-bit run
  totals, fixes streamed-court hazards/destructibles, and closes signature,
  collision, Compass, and fatal-hazard presentation edge cases. The
  [numbered audit](../bug-sweep-2026-07-28.md) identifies every live-ROM
  regression.
- Nonlinear portals and direct large-room checkpoints now arrive at the
  body-safe crossing of the guaranteed trails. This prevents a generated
  pillar from overlapping the champion's upper body and trapping every
  movement direction; the controller gate also models the cartridge's same
  horizontal and vertical full-body collision rules.
- The release-hash Picsean expedition reaches room 220 and all nine bosses in
  186,584 gameplay frames with zero combat or route stalls. A clean emulator
  independently reproduces its 187,776-frame trace at `hp=15 won=1`.

The reinforcement-caller enemy requested during this milestone is recorded as
the next encounter feature: it will telegraph line-of-sight detection and
summon a bounded, non-recursive escort wave within the fixed entity budget.
