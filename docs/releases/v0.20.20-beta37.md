# Quintra v0.20.20-beta37

Playtest build. The title screen still reads v0.20.20.

- The five heroes and the Stage 1 Sentinel have new pixel art.
- Speed Ring looks like a ring and Swift Fang looks like a fang.
- Hero select uses gold labels, the chosen hero's color, and a tighter stat row.
- Title-screen champions keep their colors, including on the MiSTer core.
- The Spirit Compass map stays blank until you walk a room or use a Chart; the GOAL and the sanctuary boss hint are earned.
- You can talk to shopkeepers from across the counter.
- Wolfkin's full-Will rush throws a frontal force wave and shields only his front for the dash.
- The victory screen no longer keeps a leftover boss silhouette or broken text after Hexa or the Void Lord.
- A print-ready manual is on the project site at https://struktured-labs.github.io/quintra/manual/.

The standalone cartridge is the quintra-gbc CI artifact and is not attached to this Release.
