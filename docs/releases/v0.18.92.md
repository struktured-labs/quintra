# Quintra v0.18.92 — Living Courts

This milestone gives every dungeon a stronger visual identity, varies the
opening shape of procedural encounters, and ships the queued reinforcement
caller.

- Crystal shards, Verdant roots, Ember braziers, Frost ice columns, Mire
  fungi, Shadow gargoyles, Golden idols, Bloodmoon bone altars, and Void eyes
  replace the formerly shared pillar/crystal/debris silhouettes.
- Those stage atlases reuse the same three semantic background IDs. Solid
  scenery remains solid and debris remains walkable, so the art pass changes
  no route, collision, puzzle, or procgen topology.
- Four deterministic permutations distribute enemies across the nine
  guaranteed-open court anchors. They add ring, flank, reverse, and crossfire
  openings without consuming another RNG draw.
- Every two-enemy turn court still places at least one body in a distant
  sector, preserving the purpose of the scrolling 31×31 field.
- The new **Rift Cantor** joins stage pools from Frost Vault onward at 6%.
  It flashes and sounds a 48-frame interruptible chant before calling one
  escort wave.
- Normal calls two stage-attuned escorts; Easy calls one. Escort IDs exclude
  the Cantor, a full entity table fails safely, and a spent caller can never
  rearm.
- Cantor art reuses the merchant OBJ tile only in hostile dungeon rooms.
  Shops and towns reload their ordinary merchant identity.
- Typed content validation now covers 33 enemies and keeps every stage pool
  at exactly 100 weight.
- Live-ROM regressions prove nine distinct scenery uploads, four distinct
  formation openings, far-field court pressure, C/Rust procgen parity, the
  Cantor warning window, Normal/Easy summon counts, non-recursion, and the
  single-wave bound.
- Riftwild rendering now refreshes world visibility at a bounded cadence,
  retires only OAM slots that were used on the preceding frame, and skips
  contact checks for sleeping offscreen enemies. Ordinary traversal holds
  180/180 sampled frames and the dense stress room holds 148/180.
- The controller pilot now recognizes zero-range counter-guard attacks and
  preserves those attacks instead of replacing them with an inward edge
  nudge. A clean Easy Picsean expedition and replay reach all-nine-boss
  Victory in 190,271 frames at `room=220 bosses=9 hp=13 won=1`.
- Riftwell regression timing now waits for the complete compact-room-to-field
  handoff before injecting the next warp, matching the cartridge's blocked
  tilemap transaction instead of racing it.

The ROM remains a 128 KiB CGB-only MBC5 + RAM + battery cartridge. Every
switchable gameplay bank retains at least the required 1 KiB development
floor; the cold stage/caller asset bank retains more than 9 KiB.
