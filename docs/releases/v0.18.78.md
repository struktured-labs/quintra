# Quintra v0.18.78 — Unbroken Paths

Consecutive 31×31 dungeon courts and Riftwild cells now cross as continuous
districts. The cartridge rotates its 32×32 hardware background map and streams
the next generated field behind the moving viewport, keeping the LCD on, the
champion visible, and the music sequencer advancing. Procedural nodes remain
independent encounters and Compass locations; only the presentation seam is
removed.

The streamer is reversible in both axes and atomically publishes the new
tilemap origin only after all 961 destination tiles are resident. Returning
from the Spirit Compass rebuilds a field at that rotated origin. Door, seal,
puzzle-block, secret, and outdoor-label writes use the same mapping.

Compact authored puzzle chambers retain the classic room slide. Boss,
village, and palette-changing boundaries retain their safe full rebuild.

Live-ROM tests cross dungeon fields west/east and south/north, traverse all
sixteen Riftwild cells, sample LCD, OAM, scroll, and music state throughout,
and compare every destination tile against physical CGB VRAM. The optimized
camera path measures 180/180 ordinary frames and 144/180 under twelve
persistent projectiles, with 1,038 bytes free in the tight gameplay bank.

An input-only Picsean campaign reaches the ending at frame 193,716 after all
nine Colossi with 15 HP remaining, collecting all eight observed post-boss
relics. The route stays within its 210,000-frame cartridge budget.
