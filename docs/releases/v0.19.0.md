# Quintra v0.19.0 — Living Dungeons

This milestone moves procedural variation from room layout into encounter
rules, makes the critical route readable, and adds one dependable cross-room
puzzle circuit to every full-size dungeon.

- First visits to eligible scrolling courts deterministically become an
  ordinary skirmish, delayed **TRAP**, two-formation **WAVE**, marked
  **ELITE** hunt, or timed **HOLD** with bounded reinforcements.
- Each special verb has a brief tile-native arrival callout and its own door
  release condition. Backtracking returns to an ordinary encounter, so a
  completed event cannot be farmed for another reward.
- Normal director enemies receive one extra durability step; Easy retains its
  inspection-oriented values. Pressure comes chiefly from timing, formations,
  optional escorts, and reinforcement cadence rather than a global HP wall.
- ELITE and HOLD completions offer two separated class-attuned relic orbs.
  The red offense and cyan sustain/utility branches are mutually exclusive;
  taking one removes the other and uses the existing run-long item system.
- The director's elite objective now owns a dedicated entity-slot handle. A
  live-ROM regression caught and removed an earlier collision with the
  universal hit-flash scratch byte.
- The Spirit Compass reveals the unseen Sigil objective and highlights the
  immediate shortest-path corridor. The corresponding in-room cardinal door
  receives the same crystal cue once it is safe to use.
- The critical route now orders Sigil, Warden, Waystone, Deep Warden, the new
  room-12 **Deep Seal** switch, and then the Colossus gate. The Pack names the
  same current goal.
- Room 13 contains the switch's remote phase wall. Its center changes visibly
  while two body-width edge detours prevent procedural fold approaches from
  creating a seed-specific softlock; the sanctuary remains the hard objective
  gate.
- The controller-only balance pilot understands the new route and physically
  solves both phase-switch variants. A 30,000-frame Easy Corvin run cleared
  two Colossi, visited five merchant rooms, made one purchase, and recorded no
  combat or route stalls.
- The complete opening smoke reaches and defeats the live first Colossus. The
  release contracts also cover all four director state machines, boon choice,
  the deep phase circuit, 460 Normal/Easy stage checkpoints, 20–30-room map
  behavior, seamless wide-room transitions, procgen parity, cartridge format,
  and bank headroom.
- The GitHub gameplay reel is recaptured from v0.19.0 and now includes a live
  generated WAVE court before the Compass, Colossus, Riftwild, village, and
  ending sequence.

The ROM remains a 128 KiB CGB-only MBC5 + 32 KiB battery-RAM cartridge. Every
switchable bank retains at least the required 1 KiB development floor.
