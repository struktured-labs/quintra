# Quintra v0.18.76 — Outer Reaches

Scrolling dungeon districts and every Riftwild cell now use a 31×31-tile
world—248×248 pixels behind the Game Boy Color viewport. That is 37% more
generated collision terrain than the former 224×200 fields, with 88 pixels of
horizontal camera travel and 112 vertical. Stage one retains eight large
districts and the finale fifteen, so the extra scale is real exploration
space rather than filler room counters.

The new far sectors carry extended ruins, stage-colored cover, encounter
aprons, and additional seed-rotated Riftwild landmarks. A simplified banked
collision branch fixes an SDCC optimizer issue exposed by the taller field,
and live-ROM tests cross both far doors, reach the complete camera range,
sweep all sixteen outdoor cells, and finish the ending route.

The complete five-champion PyBoy curriculum is regenerated for the new ROM.
Crystal's special 224×136 Colossus arena remains unchanged, as do every enemy,
boss, hero, and difficulty value.
