# Quintra v0.20.20-beta35

Playtest build. The title screen still reads v0.20.20.

- Cinder Rex is a furnace hound again, not a stretched cockroach, and it opens its jaw and strides when it breathes or stomps.
- Frost Spider, Mire Heart roots, and Blood Hydra each have their own attack poses.
- After the Void Lord, Hexa Dragmar can appear: a hidden multi-limbed colossus. Its body reads as a dark dragon, and north and south boss shots use vertical glyphs.
- Playtest fixes: a sealed secret door stays enterable once opened; hearts fill on the HUD, low HP pulses, and a refused pickup says why; the last puzzle note plays before the fanfare; talk range is shorter and ignores a turned-away A; the companion shows before its greeting; merchant, smith, and arrival-chart shelves stay sold out after you leave; crystals draw as a rooted cluster; dungeon signs keep Rift Wild in sequence; boss floor overlays stay when a district card would land.

The standalone cartridge is the quintra-gbc CI artifact and is not attached to this Release.
