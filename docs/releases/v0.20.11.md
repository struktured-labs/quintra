# Quintra v0.20.11 — Living Labyrinths

This milestone gives the procedural labyrinths more consequence, motion, and
regional identity while making the whole cartridge easier to inspect.

- Villages now own **Hearthlight Common**, a dedicated 85.3-second civic theme
  that hands off cleanly to the next dungeon score.
- A wrong ordered-rune press costs one half-heart, plays the real damage
  feedback, and resets the phrase, so sequence puzzles cannot be brute-forced
  for free.
- Frost Vault can generate amber-telegraphed wall launchers that fire fast,
  arrow-shaped hazards inward from alternating sides.
- Shadow Keep can generate two rows of cracked floor panels that warn, vanish,
  damage and safely displace a careless champion, then reform around a
  permanent central route.
- The former one-step deep switch is a seeded four-panel **Aether Lattice**.
  Every plate changes itself and its neighbors; completing the solvable lattice
  persists a phase change and opens a remote wall elsewhere in the dungeon.
- Hazard placement is run-, room-, and seed-stable and remains optional combat
  pressure rather than a procedural progression lock.
- The cartridge field archive now includes all nine stages, nine Colossi, 33
  monsters, and 29 actual authored relic/tool/Waygear sprites, plus a unified
  shareable atlas rather than invented promotional art.
- All 460 PyBoy and 460 native mGBA stage/character checkpoints are regenerated
  and verified against the tagged ROM.

The release retains v0.20.10's persistent regional Riftwild, champion-specific
geography, permanent Waygear, readable residents and merchants, dense Stage 1
fields, and guarded reachability for major rewards.
