# Quintra v0.20.20-beta34

Playtest build. The title screen still reads v0.20.20.

- Letting go of the D-pad stops you and keeps the last aim, including diagonals. Standing still and attacking fires that way until you aim somewhere new. Classic aim, in the Pack, still snaps a diagonal to up or down.
- Browser sound stays primed while you play, and a hidden tab suspends audio instead of draining it. If the banner is up, press a button or tap to turn sound on.
- Below half health, Spider, Hydra, Crystal, Mire, and the Void Lord move like themselves again instead of drifting into the east wall. The Void Lord's blast marks the square that is actually safe. Sideways boss shots face the direction they travel.
- Mire Heart can root in place and draw the path of the roots before they grow. The gaps between them are walkable, the roots are gone before the safe-center ring, and a corner will not be sealed with no way out.
- Crowded rooms run lighter. Hits and shots are unchanged.

The standalone cartridge is the quintra-gbc CI artifact and is not attached to this Release.
