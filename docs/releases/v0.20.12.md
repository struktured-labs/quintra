# Quintra v0.20.12 — Road Echoes

This milestone adds a procedural travelling-party layer inspired by *Final
Fantasy Adventure* without turning Quintra's solo combat into an escort game.

- Every expedition receives one seed- and stage-selected **Road Echo**:
  Hearth, Aether, or Way. Early expeditions rotate through all three roles.
- The authored 16×16 hooded follower has two walk frames, a role palette,
  terrain-aware pursuit, safe catch-up placement, and no blocking collision.
- Echoes occasionally aim a slow, one-damage allied bolt at a nearby visible
  enemy. Their cadence is intentionally restrained so the champion remains
  the build's main source of damage.
- SELECT's tile-native Compass exposes **A ASK**. Hearth restores one heart,
  Aether restores two MP, and Way reveals adjacent dungeon/Riftwild routes and
  grants a brief safe step.
- ASK has a 20-active-second cooldown. Menu time does not consume it, and the
  appended cooldown byte is included in suspend SRAM while preserving
  compatibility with pre-Road-Echo 51-byte run records.
- Companion logic updates on deliberate two-frame beats and dispatches
  directly from the entity loop. Verified dense-room performance remains
  above the cartridge's release floor.
- Elite and Hold directive rewards are queued until the director bank returns,
  then created atomically in the pickup bank before doors unseal. A short boon
  grace window prevents an underfoot pair from selecting itself.
- Live-ROM coverage checks all nine stage entries, both follower stride frames,
  following and catch-up, one-damage support fire, every ASK role, cooldown
  refusal/countdown, SRAM migration, directive reward safety, and downstream
  gameplay contracts.

The design deliberately takes the companion/ASK rhythm from the *Final Fantasy
Adventure* lineage while keeping Quintra's run seed, generated geography,
five champions, bullet pressure, and item builds in control of each journey.
