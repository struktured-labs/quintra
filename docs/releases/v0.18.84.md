# Quintra v0.18.84 — Whole-World Training

ROM SHA-256: `c1e130f69403eac784a4224f2454302e9c0e08c1e5d090f1767fc0fdc83f52e0`

Automated play now sees the same complete scrolling world as a person. The
framework-neutral PyBoy/RL path had retained a 160×136 viewport assumption
after dungeon fields grew to 248×248 and all nine Colossus arenas grew to
224×136. Valid enemies, hostile projectiles, pickups, and collision terrain
beyond that seam vanished from policy input.

The public observation now includes live world dimensions, camera position,
and the full collision field. Controller routing, firing lanes, pickups, and
true perimeter exits use those bounds while compact recorded observations
remain compatible. A linked-ROM regression moves the stage-four Colossus to
x=184 and proves that it remains visible and attackable in the eastern
chamber.

The corrected 45-fight Normal diagnostic observes every giant for 100% of its
live frames and clears 8/45, rather than the clipped observer's 4/45. It
survives 13/45, preserving late bosses as a focused attended-testing concern.
Remaining boss HP now comes from the last live observation instead of a false
zero after Game Over replaces the entity table.

Thirty-second Normal room samples resolve 18/45 stage entries and 30/45
mid-stage courts with zero deaths. The prior 39/45 and 44/45 figures were
inflated when the pilot treated an internal 160-pixel seam like the world's
edge.

Attended feedback that stages still feel compact remains the design authority.
The current graph owns 20–30 cells and a 19–31-transition mandatory objective
route, but direct boss distance can be only 9–21. Future geography work will
emphasize memorable subregions, route cadence, and meaningful optional depth
instead of empty room-count inflation.

No cartridge combat, encounter, topology, difficulty, economy, or reward
values change in this instrumentation milestone.
