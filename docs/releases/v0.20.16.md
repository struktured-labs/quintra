# Quintra v0.20.16 — Returning Edge

Returning Edge is the public-demo beta cut: a concentrated release of the
gameplay, presentation, world, browser, and hardware work completed after
Clear Choice.

- Waking and Hollow Riftwild share one tool-gated overworld, with counterpart
  terrain, tougher Hollow encounters, Worldglass shifting, regional relics,
  Wayfarer guidance, caves, vaults, villages, and an expanded title-linked
  score.
- All nine Colossi have distinct screen-scale bodies and tighter pacing.
  Quiet neutral floors and high-contrast palettes keep the boss and hostile
  projectiles readable, while victory music and palette survive Pack visits.
- Return routes can trigger named trap minibosses, including the enlarged
  Dread Reaper and its warned mortal shot. Late room overlays are followed by
  full-body spawn repair so monsters cannot begin inside scenery.
- Status, curse, temporary-buff, pickup, companion, stage-entry, Sigil, and
  Waygear feedback are visible without consulting external instructions.
- Merchants may carry a 30-coin Boomerang. B throws it while a hero power is
  cooling down or lacks a target; it Stops ordinary enemies, cuts hostile
  shots, retrieves loose loot, returns to the moving hero, and now has
  separate purchase, throw, and catch feedback.
- The itch.io package carries Quintra's maintained WasmBoy audio, gamepad,
  mobile-touch, dark-page, and scaling corrections.
- `make demo-kit` exports four hash-bound, cold-resumable Analogue Pocket
  stations for the first room, Boomerang merchant, Crystal Colossus, and
  Riftwild. The ordinary player ROM and save remain separate.

The release gate rebuilds the cartridge and every ROM-derived media asset,
regenerates all 460 deterministic checkpoints, checks reproducibility and
battery suspend, validates the browser package, and runs the complete
live-cartridge regression suite.
