# Quintra v0.20.7 — Broods & Warbands

This milestone gives procedural combat rooms recognizable monster cultures
without removing occasional anything-goes encounters.

- **Brood** rooms contain one stage-native species.
- **Pair** rooms alternate two compatible species.
- **Command** rooms contain one unusual leader and one dominant minion species.
- **Mixed** rooms retain the original independent weighted rolls and remain
  roughly one quarter of ordinary combat rooms.
- Trap ambushes, second waves, and Hold reinforcements inherit the room family.
- Room identity is deterministic across backtracking and suspend/resume.
- Rune Lanterns, Dread Bells, Rift Wardens, Bramble Sprites, Shard Crabs, and
  Rift Cantors can lead Command rooms or appear in Mixed rooms, but cannot fill
  an unreadable or route-blocking dense brood.
- A lone surviving Rift Ooze fragment now pursues the champion instead of
  wandering indefinitely behind cover after its twin dies.
- Mandatory three-, four-, and five-note Waystones carve a body-width circuit
  through every rune, so wide-court terrain cannot maroon one note while the
  seed-dependent phrase itself remains unknown.
- Body density, enemy HP, elite odds, stage pools, and RNG draw count are
  unchanged.
- `make pocket-test-saves` now emits the complete 460-checkpoint Analogue
  Pocket curriculum in mirrored Easy/Normal and checkpoint-family folders.
  Every independent ROM/battery-save pair cold-resumes through Quintra's real
  Continue flow before it is considered deployable.

Live-ROM coverage samples 108 procedural rooms across all nine stages, proves
all four roster grammars, retains 10–12 distinct roster signatures per stage,
and verifies themed reinforcements, density, procgen parity, cartridge budget,
and the controller-only full-game route.
