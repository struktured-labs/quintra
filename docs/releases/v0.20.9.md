# Quintra v0.20.9 — Carved Resonance

This polish milestone makes the Pack easier to scan and preserves discovery
music when rewards overlap the solve animation.

- START's Pack uses nine dedicated 2bpp frame tiles instead of `+`, `-`, and
  `|` font characters.
- Identity, Vitals, Arms, and Quest have real corners, beveled horizontal and
  vertical rails, shared section junctions, distinct palettes, and a closing
  bottom border.
- Class, difficulty, stage, Dungeon Law, stats, Will, A/B actions, current
  goal, physical tool, Oath Art, and button help still fit on one 20×18 page.
- Secret discoveries own the effect bus for their complete 45-frame,
  four-note figure.
- The shorter three-note clear arpeggio receives an 18-frame melody lock.
- Core, reward, weapon, equip, and rune voices respect both priority windows;
  the underlying pickup or combat action still happens normally.
- Live-ROM coverage opens the Pack for all five champions and verifies every
  corner, rail, junction, icon, and class-specific B reminder.
- A forced coin pickup during Echo Chime proves the coin is granted without
  replacing the fanfare's opening or its later Ab4/D5 envelopes.

The release retains v0.20.8's animated champions, adaptive score, attack
relics, expanded merchant catalog, and guarded reachable pickup placement.
