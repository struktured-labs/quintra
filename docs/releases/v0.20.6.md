# Quintra v0.20.6 — Roots & Bellows

This milestone gives the early dungeons stronger mechanical identities and
removes Wolfkin's opening one-shot problem.

- Wolfkin's normal Fang is 3 damage, down from 6 before affinity.
- One Fang arc may cleave two enemies but cannot hit one overlapping body
  twice; its 64-pixel reach and broad neutral sweep remain.
- PowerStone visibly broadens attacks, Swift Fang accelerates and gilds them,
  and VampSigil stains its strengthened attacks red.
- Swift Fang's SPD now actually reduces Wolfkin's combo cadence.
- Rune trials scale from three tones in Stage 1 to four widely spread tones in
  Stage 2 and five criss-crossed tones from Stage 3 onward.
- After each dungeon's opening four-room phrase, seeded Verdant rooms can
  contain three optional cuttable root knots; clearing the network reshapes
  lanes and pays a temporary weapon Surge.
- Seeded Ember rooms can cycle ten marked tiles through warning, ignition, a hot
  hazard phase, cooling, and a one-time Surge reward.
- Ember exploration has a rewritten eight-section, roughly 68-second score
  with a rounded reed, lower register, quieter envelope, and more rests.
- Kilnback recovery vents peel into cardinal weapon lanes. Normal retains the
  brief punish window; Easy opens it earlier and lengthens the shared hazard
  recovery epoch so later-stage inspection remains practical.

The release ROM is generated from the tagged source, passes the fixed-bank
emergency floors, and includes fresh ROM-bound developer checkpoints for every
stage, champion, difficulty, boss, Riftwild return, and village.
