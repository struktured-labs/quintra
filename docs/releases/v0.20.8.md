# Quintra v0.20.8 — Resonant Arsenal

This milestone makes the champions, their builds, the soundtrack, and every
procedural reward react more legibly without weakening the cartridge budget.

- All five champions have generated idle, two-stride walk, damage-recoil, and
  ascended sprite families.
- Genuine HP loss shows a twelve-frame hurt pose; iframes alone do not.
- Eight-direction double-tap dashes have a dedicated wind-cut sound.
- **Blast Seed** adds a real area hit around a struck monster.
- **Echo Prism** produces a bounded two-child-to-four-lane fractal pattern.
- **Rift Lens** makes every third primary a broad, connected two-part beam.
- Merchant generation now spans eight build relics by six tactical offers,
  with differentiated icons, descriptions, and runtime mechanics.
- The score phrase-latches four health tiers, enemy proximity and density,
  minibosses, merchant/sanctuary context, relative hero strength, and two
  ranges of special-item resonance without restarting stage music.
- Hearts, currency, magic, merchant wares, items, weapons, boons, Sigils,
  Riftwells, and Farfold caches all use one player-sized safe-placement guard.
- Invalid reward coordinates search deterministic nearby floor and ultimately
  fall back to the champion's reachable position rather than embedding in a
  wall, block, hazard, portal, or disconnected pocket.
- The lower court and Farfold cloister have a permanent body-width connection.
- Flutterbats validate their full body footprint and hunt out of blocked
  corners instead of leaving a mandatory combat room permanently sealed.
- Live-ROM tests cover all three attack geometries, every champion animation,
  adaptive phrase latching, the 48-seed shop catalog, and 27 generated
  shop/Sigil/cache rooms across all nine stages.
- Performance measures 166/180 loop frames in the generated opening, 179/180
  with a normalized enemy roster, and 148/180 under twelve hostile shots.

The release retains v0.20.7's deterministic Brood, Pair, Command, and Mixed
encounter grammars and the complete Easy/Normal external checkpoint curriculum.
