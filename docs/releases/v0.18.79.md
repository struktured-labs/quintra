# Quintra v0.18.79 — Second Wind

Normal remains Quintra's authored balance, but a difficult run now explains
the existing inspection assist at the moment it matters. After a real Normal
combat death, Game Over says `SELECT EASY AT HERO`; an Easy death suggests
trying another champion. No enemy, boss, projectile, hero, procedural world,
or Normal/Easy combat value changed.

All 460 native mGBA curriculum checkpoints are regenerated for this cartridge:
five champions in Normal and Easy across nine entries, scrolling courts,
sanctuaries, and live Colossi, plus eight Riftwild arrivals and both villages.
Every checkpoint family cold-loads through mGBA's independent startup path.

The continuous Easy Picsean trainer also writes native checkpoints at 5, 10,
15, 20, 25, and 30 emulated minutes; its final state reaches stage 6 with five
Colossi down and 16 HP. Generation is now transactional: it builds and
cold-loads an isolated complete generation, publishes its directory, then
atomically replaces the manifest. A failed or interrupted route leaves the
preceding verified set usable instead of deleting it or exposing a partial
replacement. An intentional invalid-emulator run confirms the published
manifest and generation remain untouched on failure.

The exact cartridge also completes the input-only nine-Colossus campaign and
replays its recorded controller stream to the ending. The victory audit's
multi-line `awk` expression is now portable across mawk and gawk instead of
rejecting a valid completed run on hosts that require trailing boolean
operators.

The compressed Spirit Compass, amber skull boss thresholds, proximity roar,
continuous dungeon/Riftwild districts, and nine screen-scale Colossi remain
unchanged from their verified v0.18.78 behavior.
