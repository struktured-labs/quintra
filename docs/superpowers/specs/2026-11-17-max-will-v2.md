# Max-Will v2: every starter MAX as strong a moment as Tide Vortex

Date: 2026-11-17. Status: approved by Carmelo ("read the walkthru, go for
it"). Source study: the max-Will attacks walkthrough Page (main 51408791,
Carbon picks). Picsean's Tide Vortex is the bar and changes only its crest
sound.

## Shared rules

1. **One recast lock.** Every champion's starter MAX uses the Tide Vortex's
   360-frame (6.00 s) lock (`picsean_vortex_slow_charge/ready_at`, checked
   once at the top of `will_fire_max`). Will still banks on its normal
   clock; during the lock A is an ordinary shot.
2. **One hit per body per beat.** Damage goes through one inert
   `PROJ_AUX_VORTEX` carrier and `combat_apply_player_shot_hit`, so weakness,
   LCK, status, Colossus caps and kill rewards are unchanged. Per-cast hit
   masks stop pierce from re-hitting the front body. Heavy bodies (elite,
   alpha, rare, Sentinel, Reaper, Bellwarden, large boss, Hexa) take at most
   6 per hit, as the vortex.
3. **Spectacle is cheap.** CGB BG palette washes (floor + wall slots, 0 OAM,
   restored through `room_status_blind_visual`), OBJ tiles 200..203 reloaded
   per cast, at most four direct OAM (32..35) drawn by `room_draw_overlays`.
   The carrier is the one entity sprite and always parks on floor.
4. **No new hot-loop hooks.** `will_maxfx_begin` sets
   `picsean_vortex_ticks`, so the art rides the Tide Vortex's existing
   dispatch: combat's vortex tick, `room_draw_overlays` and
   `entity_init_room`'s abort (`picsean_vortex_abort`). Only one champion is
   ever in a run. An extra branch in `combat_tick` measurably cost the
   Serpent fight a logic frame (`test_serpent_tail_shield`), so none is added.
   On abort or end the art restores palettes, kills the carrier and clears
   its OAM; a new run clears `will_maxfx_live`.
5. **Own voices.** No MAX plays `SFX_ROAR` any more except Picsean's cast;
   each art writes its own CH1/CH4 voice through `sfx_claim_channels` and
   respects `sfx_melody_locked`.
6. **Banks.** All new code is `src/game/will_maxfx.c`, `#pragma bank 27` (about 9.4 KB; the ROM is already 512 KB, so the bank is free; 22/23 stay untouched for other work).
   Bank 8 (`will.c`) shrinks; the other hooks are a few bytes in banks 13
   and 18.
7. **Tiers.** Will 2-4 add +1 damage per tier (as before) and +4 px of
   reach per tier. The old side-spear and extra-fan tier pieces are gone for
   Sauran, Corvin and Vespine (Carbon: cut the tier-3/4 extras); each fires
   one carrier at every tier.

## Sauran: Thunderline Bastion (52 ticks)

- f0: ochre palette quake (10 ticks), shake, first thud. A fault spear rides
  out 12 px/tick (9 diagonal) up to 12 ticks, stops at the first wall,
  leaves a cracked trail (fx cels, gone by f44) and hits each body within
  12 px of the head once for MAX+4.
- f6 / f14: pylons rise 24 px and 40 px ahead, offset +/-10 px across the
  aim (never one scanline), each with a lower thud. Standing pylons eat
  hostile shots within 10 px (not the Reaper scythe) and step rushers back.
- f44: pylons crack and slam: shake (3, 8), ochre flash, heavy drop + noise,
  one tremor hit (MAX+2) on bodies within 24 px of either pylon. f46-52
  they sink and blink out.
- Target at Will 1 (MAX 6): about 18 on a body in the lane (was 64),
  capped 6+6 on heavies.

## Vespine: Royal Stinger and Venom Sac (54 ticks)

- f0: chartreuse wash (6 ticks), magenta/chartreuse OBJ ramp, wing buzz.
  f0-6 a frontal deflect eats hostile shots within 18 px in the aim
  half-plane.
- The Royal Stinger flies 6 px/tick (4 diagonal) for up to 8 ticks and
  lodges in the first body (MAX+4, poison); two pincers close on it and nip
  neighbours within 14 px (MAX+0). No body: it plants where it stops.
- The Venom Sac rides its host (or holds its spot) and throbs faster
  (8/4/2-tick beats). f45: rupture, magenta flash, a 28 px acid burst
  (MAX+4 once per body), washes shots in the burst, adds 45 ticks to an
  active Swarm (B). Four droplets fly out on the diagonals.
- Target: about 20 plus poison on one body (was 53).

## Corvin: Murderstorm Eclipse + Night Veil (48 ticks)

- f0: the room dims to midnight indigo until f44. A Shadow Effigy (the
  carrier) holds the cast tile and eats hostile shots within 18 px until
  f32; bodies still hurt on contact.
- Four scythe-feathers fan out on the diagonals for 20 ticks (MAX+3 once
  per body), hover and glint for 8, then snap in 12 ticks onto the Raven
  Mark if it is live, else 48 px ahead.
- f40: white/violet eclipse flash, shake, MAX+7 on bodies within 20 px;
  the marked body takes +3 and its Mark gains 45 ticks. Detuned two-pulse
  bell at f40/f43 after the f0 whoosh and an f28 swell.
- Target: about 16 unmarked, 19-20 marked (was 20 on one lane).

## Wolfkin: Moonfang Cross-Rend (42 ticks)

- Beat one is the shipped Moonfang Rush (guarded dash, three claw lanes,
  Will wave crescent) plus a 6-tick violet/silver wash and two flickering
  claw scars along the dash path.
- Beat two: at f17 the crescent cels give way to cross-rend cels hanging at
  the dash apex (44 px, 31 diagonal); f28 they flash white; f34 a 16x16 X
  shears shut: MAX+3 once on each body within 20 px, metallic ring and
  upward sweep, then shatters outward. At most four direct OAM.
- Beat one's crescent loses 2 damage per piece so one body still takes
  about 18 across both beats.

## Picsean: Tide Vortex

Look, hitboxes, damage and the 360-frame lock are unchanged. At the crest
(tick 72) the second `SFX_ROAR` becomes a filtered surf crash on CH4 (about
28 frames of decay) under a three-note bubbling run on CH1 (ticks 72/75/78).
