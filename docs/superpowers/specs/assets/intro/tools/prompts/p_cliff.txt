Authentic Game Boy Color pixel art, like a 1991 handheld cutscene panel from Ultima: Runes of Virtue: very low resolution with big chunky square pixels (the whole image is 160x144 game pixels scaled up), flat colours, at most 4 colours per 8x8 area, strong dark outlines, simple dithering, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no text, no letters, no border, no UI, no frame. A lone small hero seen from behind standing on a rocky cliff edge in the lower left, looking out over a vast wild overgrown land of floating rock islands, crooked trees, ruins and rivers (the Riftwild). On the far horizon, a tall jagged glowing violet crack splits the sky. Dawn colours low on the horizon, dark sky above. Wide, epic but simple.
