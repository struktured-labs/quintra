Authentic Game Boy Color pixel art, like a 1991 handheld cutscene panel from Ultima: Runes of Virtue: very low resolution with big chunky square pixels (the whole image is 160x144 game pixels scaled up), flat colours, at most 4 colours per 8x8 area, strong dark outlines, simple dithering, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no text, no letters, no border, no UI, no frame. A crumbling stone archway standing in darkness; through the arch, and through a vertical glowing magenta tear in reality inside it, you see a bright wild overgrown meadow with floating rocks, strange flowers, crooked trees and ruins (the Riftwild). The arch is centered, the bottom of the picture is a stone path leading into the arch, empty foreground for a walking character.
