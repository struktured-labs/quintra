Authentic Game Boy Color pixel art, like a 1991 handheld cutscene panel from Ultima: Runes of Virtue: very low resolution with big chunky square pixels (the whole image is 160x144 game pixels scaled up), flat colours, at most 4 colours per 8x8 area, strong dark outlines, simple dithering, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no text, no letters, no border, no UI, no frame. A 3x3 grid of nine separate small square dungeon-entrance panels, separated by thick black gutters, each a different dungeon mouth: 1 crystal caverns (blue crystals), 2 verdant hollow (giant hollow tree, magenta flowers), 3 ember depths (volcanic skull cave, lava), 4 frost vault (ice door, snow), 5 toxic mire (swamp cave, green bubbles), 6 shadow keep (dark castle gate, purple), 7 golden temple (sun temple steps, gold), 8 bloodmoon (red moon over a ruined tower), 9 void sanctum (floating violet ruin over the void). Each panel centered on its entrance.
