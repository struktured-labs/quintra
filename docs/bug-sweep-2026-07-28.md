fix below is covered by a PyBoy cartridge regression or by the deterministic
controller trace named in the verification column.

## Certification follow-ups

The whole-run controller gate exposed additional cartridge and certification
defects after the numbered twenty were closed:

- The balance pilot modeled pillar/block body collision only while moving
  vertically, although the cartridge applies the same six-probe contract on
  both axes. Its pathfinder now rejects the same horizontal overlaps as the
  game. A resumed native checkpoint advances from room 67 through room 82
  without a combat or route stall under the corrected model.
- Nonlinear portals and direct large-room checkpoints arrived at `(72,92)`.
  That point occupied the carved vertical feet lane but not the protected
  horizontal intersection, so generated scenery could overlap the champion's
  upper half and leave every legal escape step blocked. These arrivals now use
  `(72,60)`, where the two guaranteed body-width trails cross. The exact
  pre-failure room-110 checkpoint enters and clears room 111, then reaches room
  127 in 10,000 controller-only frames with zero combat or route stalls.
- The Counter Guard controller used a coarse target route after body overlap,
  which could leave Wolfkin pinned against legal scenery overhang without a
  real Fang firing lane. It now falls back to the exact pixel-step lane search
  after overlap or 120 no-damage frames. The formerly stuck seed survives
  18,000 frames, reaches room 29, and clears its first giant.
- The vampirism regression assumed a killed enemy slot remained empty for a
  frame, but the cartridge can immediately reuse that slot for the death FX.
  The assertion now checks the actual contract—the original slot is no longer
  an active enemy—without rejecting legal effect reuse.
- Cartridge-accurate six-probe collision makes long native emulator fixtures
  substantially slower on the host. Their wall-clock allowances now cover the
  measured runtime while their gameplay frame caps and pass criteria remain
  unchanged.

`test_overworld.py` asserts the body-safe portal coordinate in the live ROM.
The final fixed-seed Picsean certification then reaches room 220 and
all-nine-boss Victory in 186,584 gameplay frames with zero combat or route
stalls; a clean emulator reproduces the 187,776-frame input trace at
`bosses=9 hp=15 won=1`.
